# Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) 411th Parliamentary Standing Committee Report (2026-27)
## Key Polar, Ocean & Climate Research Findings

### 1. Maitri-II Antarctic Replacement Research Station
- **Existing Maitri Station**: Commissioned in 1989, Maitri has far exceeded its designed operational lifespan. The station faces structural deterioration, space constraints, and limitations in waste-management/environmental compliance systems.
- **Maitri-II Establishment**: Modern replacement station in Antarctica required to ensure safety, operational sustainability, and India's continued year-round scientific presence under the Antarctic Treaty System.
- **Architectural Design Consortium**: Selected through a global design competition. The winning consortium comprises Ramboll Deutschland GmbH and bofArchitekten (Germany).
- **Polar Infrastructure Self-Reliance**: Recommendations to involve Indian engineering firms and indigenous cold-weather construction capabilities (referencing India's first station, Dakshin Gangotri, built with the Indian Army).

### 2. NCPOR & Ice-Class Polar Research Vessel (PRV)
- **National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research (NCPOR, Goa)**: Manages India's polar expeditions to Antarctica (Maitri & Bharati) and the Arctic (Himadri station in Svalbard).
- **Polar Research Vessel Acquisition**: Department of Expenditure has approved in-principle the acquisition of a dedicated ice-class Polar Research Vessel with ice-breaking capabilities at an estimated outlay of ₹2,329.40 crore.

### 3. PRITHVI Vigyan (PRITHVI) Scheme & Ocean Technologies
- Comprehensive scheme strengthening capabilities across atmosphere, oceans, cryosphere, and geosciences.
- **Desalination Plants**: Low-Temperature Thermal Desalination (LTTD) plants established in Lakshadweep islands under PRITHVI-OSMART. Transitioning from diesel generator power to renewable energy (solar, Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion OTEC).

### 4. Mission Mausam & Advanced Observational Systems
- Transformation into a weather-ready and climate-smart nation using AI-based weather tools, hyper-local forecasting, and Doppler Weather Radar (DWR) networks.
- Expansion of 84 additional Doppler Weather Radars (DWRs) across India (budget of ₹942.57 crore).
- Cloud microphysics laboratory at IITM Pune for cloudburst early warnings in North-Eastern States, Western Ghats, and Western Himalayas.
- Floating radar observation systems mounted on ocean buoys.

### 5. Deep Ocean Mission & Samudrayaan
- Approved outlay of ₹4,077 crore for deep-sea exploration, polymetallic nodule mining, marine biodiversity, and the Samudrayaan crewed submersible programme.
